Popis

4-bedroom apartment in the centre of Devínska Nová Ves. The apartment is located in an apartment building on the 6th floor, in the centre of Devínska Nová Ves. Nearby facilities include: post office, health centre, primary school, KRAJ grocery store opposite the building, pharmacy located in the shopping centre. The apartment is oriented to the north and south. The apartment building was built in 1989 and is in good, well-maintained condition. The apartment is accessible via two new elevators through a shared lockable hallway. The apartment has a practical layout, with all rooms accessible from the entrance hall or hallway. The living room provides access to the loggia as well as to the kitchen. Apartment layout and room sizes: Living room – 19.46 m², Room – 12.01 m², Room – 8.61 m², Room – 8.61 m², Kitchen – 11.04 m², Bathroom – 3.10 m², Separate toilet – 1.05 m², Entrance hall – 6.80 m², Hallway – 8.51 m², Loggia – 4.50 m², Cellar storage unit – 1.80 m². Total area: 85.49 m². Living area: 80.99 m². The rooms feature wooden parquet flooring. The interior doors and door frames are also made of solid wood. Ceramic tiles are installed in the entrance hall, hallway, kitchen and bathroom. The apartment includes a private cellar storage unit. The sale also includes a co-ownership share of the built-up land and the adjacent plot.
